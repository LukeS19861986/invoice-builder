Why InvoiceCreator Exists

Creating a professional invoice should not require learning accounting software, opening an account or paying a subscription.

InvoiceCreator is deliberately focused on one job: create a good-looking invoice, download it and get back to work.

Professional by default

The default design is restrained, readable and suitable for real client work. Profession-aware details are intentionally subtle rather than decorative.

Private by design

Invoice details and logos are processed inside your browser. InvoiceCreator does not need to store your customer or billing data.

One tool. One job.

No dashboard, CRM, client database or accounting suite. If a feature does not make creating the invoice easier, it stays out of the way.
