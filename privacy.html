Privacy Policy

Privacy is part of how InvoiceCreator works.

Invoice information, client details and uploaded logos are handled locally in your web browser for the purpose of creating the invoice and PDF. InvoiceCreator does not require those details to be uploaded to or stored on an InvoiceCreator server.

Analytics

InvoiceCreator may use ordinary website analytics to understand general website traffic, browser/device types, approximate location and page interactions. Analytics does not receive the contents of your invoice fields or uploaded logo.

Third-party resources

The website may load fonts and JavaScript libraries from third-party content delivery services. Those providers may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type when resources are requested.
