How InvoiceCreator Works

InvoiceCreator helps freelancers, contractors and small businesses create professional PDF invoices directly in a web browser.

Your invoice details, client information and logo stay in your browser while you work. InvoiceCreator does not need an account or a database to create your document.

Choose the kind of work you do

Your profession changes only a few sensible defaults and a very subtle visual detail. The invoice itself remains clean and professional.

Add the invoice information

Enter your business details, client, dates, line items, rates, optional tax, discount, payment information and notes.

Add a logo if you want

Your logo is read locally in the browser and used in the invoice preview and PDF. It is not uploaded to InvoiceCreator.

Preview and download

Preview the finished invoice, make any changes you need and download the result as a PDF.

Privacy

InvoiceCreator does not store your invoice data on a server. The tool does not automatically save the working invoice. Only your optional business profile or explicitly exported draft is retained. Your browser may separately restore form values or a previous tab.

Remembering your business

Nothing is saved automatically by the tool. If you choose “Remember my business on this device”, sender details, your logo and payment instructions are saved in this browser’s local storage. Client details, invoice items and notes are excluded. Use “Clear saved business” or uncheck the option to remove this saved profile. Clearing it leaves the currently open invoice available to edit. Avoid this option on shared devices. Private browsing or clearing site data may remove the profile.

Editable local drafts

Export editable draft downloads a JSON file containing your entire invoice, including client and payment details. Keep that file private. Import editable draft reads one of these files locally and replaces the current invoice only after confirmation. It does not upload the file or automatically remember its business details.

Amounts and required fields

Enter quantities with up to four decimal places and rates with two. Each line is rounded half up to cents; the currency amount discount is subtracted before tax, which is rounded once. Discount cannot exceed subtotal. Business name, client name, invoice number, invoice date, due date and item descriptions are required. Optional email addresses must be valid. Logo formats are PNG, JPEG and WebP, up to 4 MB; logos are resized locally. Invoice numbers include a random suffix to reduce duplicates, remain editable, and should be checked against your own records.
