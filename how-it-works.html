How InvoiceTool Works

InvoiceTool helps freelancers, contractors and small businesses create professional PDF invoices directly in a web browser.

Your invoice details, client information and logo stay in your browser while you work. InvoiceTool does not need an account or a database to create your document.

Choose the kind of work you do

Your profession changes only a few sensible defaults and a very subtle visual detail. The invoice itself remains clean and professional.

Add the invoice information

Enter your business details, client, dates, line items, rates, optional tax, discount, payment information and notes.

Add a logo if you want

Your logo is read locally in the browser and used in the invoice preview and PDF. It is not uploaded to InvoiceTool.

Preview and download

Preview the finished invoice, make any changes you need and download the result as a PDF.

Privacy

InvoiceTool does not store your invoice data. Closing or refreshing the page clears the working information from the browser session.
